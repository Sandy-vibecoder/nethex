Access points

Seen by the radio. Walk mode scans every 30 s and tags each reading with every AP it can hear, so the AP and Best AP layers fill in as you walk. Android caps scans at 4 per 2 minutes.

Live devices —

Every 15 s the listener pings — and reads the ARP table, which answers even for sleeping phones. Online means it answered this sweep; a device is marked gone after 3 missed sweeps. This is who is on the network, not where they are.
